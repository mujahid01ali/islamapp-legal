This Privacy Policy explains what information the Islam app (the “App”) collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. We aim to collect as little personal information as possible, and every optional field is exactly that — optional.

Information We Collect

• Account identity: if you choose to sign in with Apple or Google, we receive a unique account ID and, depending on what you share with that provider, your name and email address.

• Optional profile details: if you choose to fill them in, your name, email, phone number, city, country, age, and gender. None of these are required to use the App.

• App activity synced to your account (only if signed in): tasbih (dhikr) counts, and Qur'an reading/listening progress (which surahs and ayahs you've reached), so this data can follow you across your devices.

• Location: with your permission, your device's location is used on-device to calculate prayer times and Qibla direction. Location is not stored on our servers or sent to any third party by the App.

• Notifications: if enabled, used only to send local prayer-time reminders on your device.

How We Use Information

Information you provide is used solely to operate the App's features: authenticating your account, syncing your progress across devices, and personalizing the experience. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where Data Is Stored

Signed-in account data (profile, tasbih progress, reading progress) is stored using Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database), a third-party cloud service. Access to your data in our database is restricted to your own account. If you do not sign in, this data stays only on your device.

Your Choices

You can use the App without signing in or filling in any profile details. If signed in, you can edit or clear your profile at any time in Settings, and you can permanently delete your account and all associated synced data from Settings → Delete Account. Deleting your account is irreversible.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated “Last updated” date above.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy, or requests to access or delete your data, can be sent to support@islamknowledge.app.
