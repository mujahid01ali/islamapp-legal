Religious Content

The Qur'anic text, translations, transliterations, Hadith, and dua/adhkar content in this App are provided for personal reference and study. Translations are, by their nature, an interpretation of the original Arabic and may not capture every nuance of meaning. Hadith gradings and attributions, and translator/collection credits, are shown as provided by their sources. This content has not been independently certified by a religious authority, and should not be treated as a substitute for consulting a qualified scholar (‘alim) on matters of religious rulings (fiqh) or interpretation.

Prayer Times & Qibla

Prayer times are calculated using standard astronomical formulas and a calculation method you select; results depend on your device's location accuracy and can vary slightly from times published by local mosques or authorities, which may account for local sighting or adjustment conventions. For matters where precise timing is important, please verify against a local, authoritative source.

Third-Party Sources

Qur'an text, translations, and recitation audio are retrieved from the Quran.com Content API, and Hadith collections from the fawazahmed0 Hadith API. We are not the publisher of this content and are not responsible for errors, changes, or availability of these third-party sources.
