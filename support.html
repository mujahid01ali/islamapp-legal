Account & data

To delete your account and all synced data, open the app and go to Settings → Delete Account. This is permanent. If you're having trouble with sign-in or sync, email us with the email/provider you used to sign in and a description of the issue.

Reporting a content issue

If you notice an error in a translation, Hadith reference, or prayer-time calculation, please email us with the specific surah/ayah, Hadith, or location/date involved so we can look into it.
